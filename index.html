HOW TO PLAY
01
THE GAME
Two degenerates. One die. Take turns rolling until someone reaches the goal. Loser buys the drinks.
02
ROLL [SPACE]
Roll the die. The result adds to your current turn total. Keep rolling to stack points.
03
BUST × roll a 1 ×
Snake eye. You lose every point accumulated this turn. Nothing. Zero. Turn passes.
04
HOLD [ENTER]
Bank your current points to your total score and pass the turn. The safe play — if you're a coward.
05
WIN
First player to reach or exceed the goal score wins. The house pays out. The loser pays in shame.
