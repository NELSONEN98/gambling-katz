Three families, each pulling its own shift. Pixelify Sans handles the marquee signs, character names, and anything that needs retro-arcade energy — it sets the scene. 09 — Foundation

Design Principles

Decisions made on purpose. Each principle is a constraint that shapes the visual system.

01

Atmosphere First

The table must feel alive before the player makes a single move. Smoke, scanlines, felt grain, and neon flicker are not decoration — they are the product. Lose the atmosphere and you lose the game.

02

Risk Made Visible

Every decision in the game is a risk. The UI mirrors this — a bust flashes red, a winning streak glows amber, and the inactive player dims. State is never ambiguous. The player must feel the stakes.

03

Character-Led

The animals are not cosmetic skins. Tax Penguin, Bearnardo, Lucky Ducky, and Mystery Egg each carry a personality that bleeds into their lore, their loss quotes, and their idle animations.

04

Pixel Meets Neon

Pixelated rendering meets glowing shadows. The combination creates deliberate tension — old-school fidelity colliding with neon excess. It defines the visual language and cannot be separated.
