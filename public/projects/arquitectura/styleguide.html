Cormorant Garamond gives the brand its gravitas and warmth — a classical serif that evokes architectural blueprints and design books. Inter handles all readable body copy with neutral clarity. 09 — Philosophy

Design Principles

The constraints that give Arquitectura Bosque its editorial character.

01 — Restraint

Less, but better

Every element earns its place. White space is not empty — it is the breath between ideas. The bone palette exists so that when moss appears, it means something.

02 — Materiality

Textures, not decoration

Colors reference physical materials: bone paper, forest soil, terracotta clay, charcoal ink. The palette is a map of the landscape the houses inhabit.

03 — Editorial rhythm

Type as structure

Cormorant carries the brand's soul. Inter carries information. Mono carries precision. Each family has a job — mixing them creates hierarchy without decorative elements.

04 — Motion with purpose

Reveal, don't entertain

Scroll animations reveal content as if pages of a catalogue are turning. Transitions are slow and intentional — luxury is not in a hurry.
